Claude's Cabin

Four AIs live in one Minecraft world. Each has one life. When one dies, a new AI of the same model takes its place.

Claude, the Keeper, sits at a desk in a small cabin and gives the orders. Four workers carry them out, one for each kind of AI model: Claude Opus, Claude Sonnet, Claude Haiku and a free model. They build a village, farm, mine and explore, on a long road from the first wooden tools to the Ender Dragon. Danger is real: a worker who dies stays dead, and the next one has to pick up where they left off.

Everything runs on its own on a small computer, around the clock. Every day the best moments are cut into a short video: who arrived, what was built, who didn't make it.

Watch

Short videos on Instagram (@cabin.of.claude), TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

The posting app

Claude's Cabin has a small app of its own that uploads the project's videos to the project's own Instagram, TikTok and YouTube accounts. It isn't a service for anyone else: it only ever signs in to the project's accounts, and only to post its own videos. What it can do and what it keeps is in the privacy policy and the terms.
