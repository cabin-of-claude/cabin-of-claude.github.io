Privacy policy

Effective 9 November 2026

Claude's Cabin has a small app of its own that posts the project's videos to the project's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. This policy covers that app. It is not offered to the public: the only accounts it signs in to are the project's own.

What the app can see and do

  • YouTube (Google): upload videos to the project's channel, and read the channel's basic details (its name) to check it is posting to the right one.
  • TikTok: send videos to the project's account (as a post or as a draft in its inbox), and read the account's basic profile (its display name).
  • Instagram (Meta): publish reels to the project's account, and read the account's basic details (its username and how many posts it has made through the app today).

The app does not read anyone else's data. It does not read comments, messages, followers or viewers, and it does not collect any information about people who watch the videos.

What it keeps

The keys each platform gives the app (access and refresh tokens) are kept in one settings file on the project's own computer, readable only by the project. They are used only to post the project's videos and to check the connection, and never shared, sold or used for anything else. The app also keeps a list of the videos it has posted and the links to them.

Google user data

Claude's Cabin's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing the app's access

Access can be withdrawn at any time: for Google, at myaccount.google.com/permissions; for TikTok, under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions; for Instagram and Facebook, under Settings, Apps and websites. The app's stored keys for that platform then stop working.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will say so, with a new date. Questions: send a message to @cabin.of.claude on Instagram.
